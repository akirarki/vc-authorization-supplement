# Supplementary Material

Supplementary material for the paper "Compile Once or Check Every Action? A Controlled Comparison of LLM Authorization under Explicit Static Permissions".

- `prompts/`: prompt texts of the two strategies
- `methodology.md`: brief methodology and column definitions
- `results/`: outcome and cost summaries for Studies 1 and 2
- `evaluation/`: the examples shown in Figures 2 and 3
